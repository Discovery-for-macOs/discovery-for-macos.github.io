Discovery for Mac

Nord Lead 2 inspired virtual analog synthesizer. Craft rich, evolving sounds with precision emulation, 32-voice polyphony, and a morphing engine. Over 3,500 presets across 40 banks, multi-layering, built-in arpeggiator, and effects including chorus, phaser, delay, and multiple distortion modes.

Modern Oscillator Engine

New DSP implementation reduces aliasing while enhancing analog warmth. Pulse width smoothing eliminates zipper noise, improved FM depth unlocks expressive modulation, and fixed PWM distortion ensures clean performance at extreme settings.

Intuitive Layer Control

MIDI channel 5 broadcasts to all layers simultaneously. Multi-layering with bank browser and 128 patch memories lets you stack complex soundscapes effortlessly. 32-voice polyphony handles dense arrangements without compromise.

Enhanced Workflow

Filter display shows precise Hz values. Gate sequencer with effect presets, click-and-drag toggling, and reliable step capture. Built-in arpeggiator, MIDI clock sync in standalone mode, and output limiter prevent clipping on hot signals.

Refined macOS Experience

Native Cocoa menus, enlarged LED controls with pointer cursors, and sandbox-compliant data storage in ~/Library/Application Support/discoDSP/Discovery/. Seamless integration for music production on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

How It Works

1. Load and Layer

Browse 40 banks with over 3,500 presets. Stack up to 32 voices across layers for intricate, morphing timbres inspired by Nord Lead 2 analog design.

2. Shape and Modulate

Tweak oscillators with smooth PWM, deep FM, and reduced aliasing. Apply filters with real-time Hz readout, then layer effects like phaser, chorus, delay, and distortion.

3. Sequence and Sync

Engage the arpeggiator or gate sequencer with preset rhythms. MIDI clock auto-detects BPM standalone; channel 5 controls all layers at once.

4. Export and Integrate

Output limiter ensures clean masters. Drag presets into your DAW or run standalone—fully optimized for macOS music workflows.
